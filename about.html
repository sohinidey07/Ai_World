About AI World

Making Artificial Intelligence easier to discover, understand and explore.

Who We Are

AI World is a student project created by AIML students as part of our DST class project. Our goal is to introduce users to different Artificial Intelligence tools through a simple, attractive and user-friendly website.

Our Mission

Helping people discover the possibilities of AI.

📚

Learn

Introduce students to AI tools and their applications.

🚀

Explore

Discover AI tools for education, creativity and productivity.

💡

Innovate

Encourage curiosity and creative thinking about technology.

🌐

Connect

Provide a simple directory of useful AI resources.

Our Project Technology

This website is designed using HTML5 and CSS3 only. It demonstrates webpage structure, navigation, layouts, styling, hover effects and responsive design. No database, backend or API is used.
